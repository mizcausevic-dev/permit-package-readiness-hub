# Origin

Permit readiness is not just a PM checklist problem. It is a revision-control, utility-coordination, approval, and inspection-packet problem. The moment drawings shift or responses come back from the jurisdiction, teams need a surface that maps package pressure to readiness evidence and the next safe move.

We built `permit-package-readiness-hub` to make that operating layer explicit. The point is not to replace a permit portal or construction management suite. The point is to show what a Construction Tech operator surface should look like when the audience needs to manage package pressure, downstream blockers, and inspection-facing posture with real schedule consequences attached.
